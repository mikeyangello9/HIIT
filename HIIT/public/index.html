PREMADE EXERCISES

  • Pushups
  • 30
  • Rest:10s

DESCRIPTION

Begin in a plank position with hands slightly wider than shoulder-width apart and arms fully extended. Lower your body towards the ground by bending your elbows while keeping your back flat and core engaged. Lower until your chest nearly touches the ground, then push through your palms to return to the starting position, fully extending your arms. Keep your body in a straight line throughout the movement, engaging your chest, shoulders, and triceps.

  • Burpees
  • 30
  • Rest:10s

DESCRIPTION

Start standing with feet hip-width apart. Lower into a squat position, placing your hands on the ground in front of you. Jump your feet back into a plank position, perform a pushup, and then jump your feet back to the squat position. From there, explosively jump up into the air with arms raised overhead. Land softly and immediately lower back into the squat position to begin the next repetition.

  • Jumping Jacks
  • 30
  • Rest:10s

DESCRIPTION

Start standing with feet together and arms by your sides. Jump into the air while simultaneously spreading your legs to the sides and raising your arms above your head. Land softly and immediately reverse the motion, jumping back to the starting position with feet together and arms by your sides. Continue this fluid motion, alternating between jumping with legs together and apart.

  • Bicycle crunches
  • 30
  • Rest:10s

DESCRIPTION

Begin lying on your back with knees bent and hands behind your head, elbows wide. Lift your head, neck, and shoulders off the ground, engaging your core. As you do, bring your right elbow towards your left knee while simultaneously extending your right leg straight and lifting it off the ground.

  • Lunges
  • 30
  • Rest:10s

DESCRIPTION

Start by standing with feet hip-width apart. Step forward with your right leg, lowering your body until both knees are bent at a 90-degree angle. Keep your front knee directly above your ankle and your back knee hovering just above the ground. Push through your right heel to return to the starting position and repeat on the other side, alternating legs for each repetition.

  • squats
  • 30
  • Rest:10s

DESCRIPTION

Begin by standing with feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly turned out. Lower your hips back and down as if sitting into a chair, keeping your chest lifted and core engaged. Lower until your thighs are parallel to the ground, then push through your heels to return to the starting position, squeezing your glutes at the top.

  • Jump squats
  • 30
  • Rest:10s

DESCRIPTION

Start in a squat position with feet hip-width apart, then explosively jump straight up into the air, extending your legs fully. Land softly back into the squat position, absorbing the impact through your legs, and immediately explode back up into another jump. Repeat for the desired number of repetitions, focusing on height and power in each jump.

  • Box Jumps
  • 30
  • Rest:10s

DESCRIPTION

Begin in a plank position with hands directly under your shoulders and body forming a straight line from head to heels. Engage your core and bring your right knee towards your chest while keeping your hips level. Quickly switch legs, bringing your left knee towards your chest while extending your right leg back. Continue alternating legs in a running motion, maintaining a steady pace and breathing rhythm.

  • Sit-ups
  • 30
  • Rest:10s

DESCRIPTION

Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat on the ground, hip-width apart. Place your hands behind your head or crossed over your chest. Engage your core as you lift your torso towards your thighs, curling up off the ground. Lower back down with control, returning to the starting position to complete one repetition.

  • Planks
  • 30
  • Rest:10s

DESCRIPTION

Begin in a plank position with elbows directly beneath your shoulders and forearms resting on the ground. Engage your core and straighten your body into a straight line from head to heels, balancing on your toes. Hold this position, focusing on maintaining tension in your core and breathing steadily. Aim to hold the plank for the desired duration, gradually increasing the time as your strength improves.
